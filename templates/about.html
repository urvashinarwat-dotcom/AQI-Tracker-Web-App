ℹ️ About AQI Tracker

A simple web application for monitoring air quality.

🌍 What is AQI?

Air Quality Index (AQI) is a numerical scale used to communicate how clean or polluted the air is and what associated health effects may be of concern.

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to provide users with an easy way to search for air quality information, understand pollution levels, and receive general recommendations based on AQI.
