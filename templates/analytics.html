Understand air quality levels and pollution categories.

AQI Categories

🟢 Good

0 - 50

Air quality is considered good. Outdoor activities are generally suitable.

🟡 Moderate

51 - 100

Air quality is acceptable, although sensitive people may experience effects.

🟠 Unhealthy for Sensitive Groups

101 - 150

Sensitive individuals should consider reducing prolonged outdoor activities.

🔴 Unhealthy

151 - 200

Everyone may begin to experience health effects.

🟣 Very Unhealthy

201 - 300

Health alert conditions may affect everyone.

🟤 Hazardous

301+

Health warnings of emergency conditions. Everyone is likely to be affected.

💡 How to Read AQI

A lower AQI value generally indicates better air quality, while a higher value indicates greater levels of air pollution.

Use the AQI category and pollutant readings together to understand the overall air quality.
